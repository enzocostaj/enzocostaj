## About me

I build software that runs in production for real clients. I currently own three products for **AquaTrade**, a seafood trading company, end to end: gathering requirements directly with the client, development, deployment and support.

I am also a co-founder of **DOMO** and have taught **competitive programming** to around 60 students.

## What I'm building

| Product | What it is | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Salmon Premium** | Warehouse management (WMS) and sales system · **in production** | 15+ modules · ~120 boxes logged per week · per-box tracking with GS1 barcodes and Zebra TC22 scanners · PDF and Excel reports |
| **OceanTrade** | B2B platform for salmon contracts and shipments · in testing | Node.js API with 11 modules and 37 tables · immutable audit trail · 150+ automated tests · Docker Compose |
| **Índice Pescados** | Weekly export price index for Chilean salmon (USD/kg) · in development | Built from 711 real shipments · available in 3 languages |
| **DOMO** | Self-service ordering and QR code payment for restaurants | Co-founder |
